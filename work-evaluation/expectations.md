## How the weighing works

The components below come from [your organization's job profile or competency framework; if it does not have one, make your own list of the responsibilities of your role]. Per component there is the expectation at my level, and the assessment from the last conversation on three levels: below expectations, meets expectations and above expectations ("that extra step").

I weigh my work against the expectation for the component, not against the learning goals. Good work that belongs to the role meets expectations, and that is a perfectly fine outcome.

Per component I keep two lists, filled from the daily reflection:

- **Above expectations ✅**: `- [date](link to daily journal): what I did and why it goes beyond the expectation. (source of recognition)`. The source of recognition is 👥 who reflected it back, 🪞 myself, or 🤖 the journal.
- **Dropped balls ⚠️**: `- [date](link to daily journal): what went wrong. Fix: what I will do differently next time.`
