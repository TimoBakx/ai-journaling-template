# 2025 Development Goals

**Date:** January 2025  
**Type:** Annual Review & Development Planning

## Performance Summary

Overall performance is strong. Technical skills are solid - code quality, problem-solving, and delivery are all at a good level. The focus for 2025 is on developing core/soft skills to complement the strong technical foundation.

## Development Goals for 2025

### 1. Improve Symfony Expertise

**Current State:**  
Good working knowledge of Symfony for day-to-day tasks. Can build features and solve problems effectively.

**Goal:**  
Deepen understanding of advanced Symfony features, patterns, and best practices. Become the go-to person for Symfony questions within the team.

**Action Items:**
- Study advanced Symfony documentation and patterns
- Experiment with less-used Symfony components
- Share learnings with the team through documentation or presentations
- Contribute to team's Symfony knowledge base

**Success Indicators:**
- Able to explain complex Symfony concepts to teammates
- Proactively suggests Symfony best practices during code review
- Implements features using advanced Symfony patterns appropriately

### 2. More Active Participation in Meetings

**Current State:**  
Tends to listen more than speak in meetings. Sometimes holds back ideas or questions, even when they would be valuable to the discussion.

**Goal:**  
Become more comfortable sharing ideas, asking questions, and contributing to discussions proactively - even when not 100% certain.

**Action Items:**
- Set a goal to ask at least one question or share one idea per meeting
- Prepare talking points before important meetings
- Practice speaking up earlier in discussions rather than waiting
- Remember that questions and half-formed ideas can spark valuable discussions

**Success Indicators:**
- Regularly contributing ideas and questions in team meetings
- Teammates reference or build upon your suggestions
- Feeling more comfortable speaking up without complete certainty
- Receiving positive feedback about meeting contributions

## Strengths to Maintain

- **Code Quality**: Continues to deliver clean, well-tested, maintainable code
- **Reliability**: Consistently meets commitments and deadlines
- **Collaboration**: Works well with teammates, helpful in code reviews
- **Problem Solving**: Systematic approach to debugging and solving technical challenges

## Manager Notes

Indy is a solid developer with strong technical skills. The focus on soft skills development will help unlock the next level of impact - not just writing good code, but also influencing technical direction and helping the team make better decisions through active participation.
